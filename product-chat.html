FAQ

Questions about the Chat Agent

Can my team reply to customers?

Yes. Replies show up in the dashboard, and anyone on your team can respond.

Does it work with my existing WhatsApp number?

In most cases, yes. We help you move the number to the WhatsApp Business API during setup.

Is it included in my plan?

Follow-ups are included on Growth and Enterprise. On Starter they're available as an add-on.
