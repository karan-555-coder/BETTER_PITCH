FAQ

Billing questions

What counts as a minute?

Connected talk time, billed per minute. Unanswered and busy calls are free.

What happens if I use more minutes?

Your campaigns keep running. Extra minutes are charged at your plan's rate on the next invoice.

Is GST included?

Prices shown are before GST. Your invoice shows GST separately, so you can claim input credit.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.
