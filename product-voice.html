FAQ

Questions about the Voice Agent

Will customers know it's an AI?

The agent introduces itself as a virtual assistant from your company, and customers can ask for a person at any time.

Which phone numbers do calls come from?

We can use your existing numbers through your telecom provider, or set up new numbers for you.

What if the agent doesn't know an answer?

It takes a message, tells the caller someone will follow up, and flags the question so you can add the answer to its knowledge base.

Do you follow DND and calling-hour rules?

Yes. Numbers are checked against DND lists, and calls only go out in permitted hours.
