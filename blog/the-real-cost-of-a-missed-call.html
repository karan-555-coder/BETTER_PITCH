The real cost of a missed call

BetterPitch team · 5 min read

Most owners know they miss some calls. Very few know what those calls are worth. Here's a quick way to put a number on it using figures you already have.

Step 1: Count the calls you miss

Your phone provider or call log shows unanswered calls. Look at a normal month and note how many inbound calls went unanswered or to voicemail. Include calls that came in after hours.

Step 2: Estimate how many were new customers

Not every missed call is a lost sale. Some are suppliers, some are existing customers who will call back. Go through a sample of 20 missed numbers and see how many were new. That gives you a rough percentage.

Step 3: Apply your close rate and job value

Of the new callers you do answer, what share turn into paying jobs? Multiply that by your average job value.

Missed revenue ≈ missed calls × % new callers × close rate × average job value

For example: 80 missed calls × 50% new × 40% close rate × ₹4,000 per job comes to ₹64,000 a month. Most people who do this for the first time find the number is bigger than they expected.

Step 4: Remember most people don't leave a voicemail

Many callers hang up and call the next business on the list. A voicemail you return two hours later often reaches someone who has already booked elsewhere.

What to do about it

  • Forward overflow and after-hours calls to a service that always answers
  • Text back missed callers automatically within a minute
  • Review your missed-call log every week, not every quarter
