Five signs your front desk is overloaded

BetterPitch team · 4 min read

An overloaded front desk rarely announces itself. It shows up as small problems that are easy to blame on something else. Here are five to watch for.

1. The phone rings while someone is being served in person

If your receptionist regularly has to choose between the customer at the counter and the one on the phone, someone is getting a worse experience every time.

2. Voicemails pile up by lunchtime

A growing list of callbacks means calls are coming in faster than they can be handled. The longer they wait, the fewer of them turn into business.

3. Booking mistakes are increasing

Double bookings, wrong times and missing details are usually a sign of rushing, not carelessness.

4. Reviews mention "hard to reach"

Customers are polite about it, but comments like "took a while to get through" are a clear signal.

5. Nobody has time to follow up

Quotes go unchased and past customers aren't reminded about their next service. That's revenue left on the table.
