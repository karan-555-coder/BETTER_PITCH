Privacy Policy

Sample text for a demo project. This is not legal advice and is not a real policy.

1. Who we are

BetterPitch ("we", "us") provides AI voice and chat agents for businesses. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number and company information you give us.
  • Call and chat data: recordings, transcripts and summaries of conversations handled for our customers.
  • Usage data: how you use our dashboard, such as pages viewed and settings changed.

3. How we use it

We use this information to provide and improve the service, support customers, send service messages and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run BetterPitch (for example hosting and telephony partners), under contracts that protect it, or when the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

Recordings and transcripts are kept for as long as the customer's settings specify, and are deleted when an account closes, unless we must keep them longer by law.

6. Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing privacy@betterpitch.example.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date below and, for significant changes, notify customers by email.
